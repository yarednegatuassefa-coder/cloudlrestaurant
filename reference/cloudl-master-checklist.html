Cloud L Restaurant

Master Checklist – 235 Items · Prioritized · Design-First

235 items
Core Design Rule: Every new element must match the existing elegant design of Cloud L (deep navy + warm terracotta/peach, same fonts, spacing, and border radius). Nothing should look forced, loud, or like a later addition. Buttons, cart, payment sections and filters must feel premium and seamless.
